About Me

Hi, I'm Giorgi. I'm a hardware engineer. Most of my work is designing PCBs and writing firmware for STM32 microcontrollers. I've also worked on FPGAs, writing the designs in Verilog.

A big part of that has been IoT boards for HVAC systems in buildings. The boards connect HVAC units to the BMS platform, so people can monitor and control them from a website. They can check temperatures, change modes, turn units on and off, that kind of thing.

Some of my personal projects are on the Home page, and there's more on my GitHub.
